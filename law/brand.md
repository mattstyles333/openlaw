# Northwind Coffee — brand claims

Fictional independent coffee roaster and cafe. Replace this file on fork.

Public-facing copy (web, menus, bag cards, agent replies, ads) MUST
match this file. If a claim is not listed under **We do make**, do not
make it. Inventing a claim is inventing policy.

## We do make

- We roast **single-origin** coffees. Each bag names the origin.
- We roast in **small batches**. We do not claim industrial scale.
- We **publish roast dates** on every bag. The date on the bag matches
  the roast log (see `constraints.md` and `sor.md`).
- We are an independent roaster and cafe. We are not a chain and we do
  not pretend to be one.

## We do not make

- Medical or health benefits of coffee. No "cures", no "detox", no
  unsourced antioxidant theatre.
- Unsubstantiated awards. If a competition result is not in this file
  (it is not — this is a template), do not cite it.
- Origin romance that is not in the roast log: invented farms, invented
  altitudes, invented producer names.
- Price comparisons, discounts, or scarcity claims unless an owner has
  written them into this week's `priorities.md` or a decided ADR.

When an agent is asked to write marketing copy, it MUST stay inside
**We do make** and MUST refuse **We do not make**. If the brief needs a
new claim, propose a decision; do not ship the claim first.
